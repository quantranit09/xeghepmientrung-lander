## Landing Page Alignment

The landing page now matches the ad group:

- URL contains `xe-ghep-da-nang-quang-tri`
- Title and H1 use `Xe ghép Đà Nẵng - Quảng Trị`
- Primary CTA says `Kiểm tra xe ghép`
- Form appears near the top of the page
- Form submits `service_type: Xe ghép Đà Nẵng - Quảng Trị`
- Page still offers xe riêng / bao chuyến when the user's schedule needs more control

## Review Rhythm

First 7 days:
- Run exact + phrase only.
- Check search terms every 2-3 days.
- Add negatives for irrelevant searches immediately.
- Keep queries with real calls/Zalo/form submits.

After 15-30 conversions:
- Test broad match in a separate experiment or ad group.
- Keep Smart Bidding only if conversion tracking is stable.
- Split high-volume destination terms into separate ad groups when they get enough traffic.

## Notes

Do not promise fixed seats, fixed departures, or fixed prices unless the operation can fulfill that exactly. Use wording like `kiểm tra chuyến phù hợp`, `xác nhận chỗ`, `gửi lịch trình` and `báo giá trước khi đi`.
